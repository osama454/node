Central Force Particle Trajectory Simulation

Equation of motion: \( \vec{F} = -k\frac{m}{r^2}\hat{r} \)

Where:

  • \( \vec{F} \) is the force vector
  • \( k \) is the force constant
  • \( m \) is the mass of the particle
  • \( r \) is the distance from the center
  • \( \hat{r} \) is the unit vector pointing from the center to the particle
